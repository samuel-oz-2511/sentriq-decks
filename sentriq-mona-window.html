The Product
One signal. Recruiters decide.
Sentriq surfaces a trust signal. The recruiter sees context and decides.
Layer 1
Behavioral Baseline
Captured as the form is filled, before identity is declared. Anonymous signals from the first keystroke.
→
Layer 2
Async Challenge
Sent post-submit. Calibrated to role. Zero friction for authentic applications. Only high-signal applications are challenged.
→
Layer 3
Human Review
Only flagged applications escalate. Recruiter sees the trust signal, not a verdict. Human judgment stays in the loop.
Gap Assessor
Reads every transcript and assignment. Surfaces gaps. Triggers a personalized async challenge. Full audit trail through offer.
